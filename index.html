MyMP

Live data · UK Parliament

Your MP's committees, votes, money and contact details — in one place.

Data comes live from UK Parliament's public Members API. Financial interests come from the Register of Members' Financial Interests. Campaign spending (as opposed to donations) isn't included — that dataset is only published as bulk CSV downloads by the Electoral Commission, not a live API.

Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0. MyMP is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the UK Parliament. Spotted something wrong? Report an issue.
